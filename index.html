What it does

Six things Sidecar handles while you work the queue.

Every tool exists because we needed it ourselves. No dashboards for the sake of dashboards — just less time per ticket.

summarize

Ticket summaries

Long threads collapsed into what happened, what's been tried, and what's still broken — before you even open the ticket.

analyze

Log analysis

Paste in logs or connect a source. Sidecar picks out the errors that matter and correlates them with the reported symptoms.

draft

Suggested replies

Replies drafted in your team's tone, based on the actual diagnosis — not generic "have you tried restarting" boilerplate.

recall

Similar incidents

Finds past tickets that match the one you're holding, including how they were resolved and how long it took.

watch

Vendor changelog monitoring

Watches the release notes of the software your users depend on and warns you before an update becomes a ticket flood.

file

Automatic Jira tickets

When an issue needs engineering, Sidecar files the Jira ticket with the evidence attached — repro, logs, and affected users.

How it works

Bolted onto your helpdesk, not a replacement for it.

Step 1

Connect

Plug Sidecar into your helpdesk and ticketing tools. No migration, no new inbox — it attaches to what you already use.

Step 2

Ride along

Sidecar appears next to each ticket with a summary, log findings, similar incidents, and a drafted reply.

Step 3

You decide

Nothing is sent or filed without a human saying so. Your engineers approve, edit, or ignore — Sidecar learns either way.
